# Privacy and legal handover

Reviewed on 8 October 2026 against the actual d3CRM v3 implementation. The public drafts are `/privacy`, `/terms`, `/data-processing`, and the standalone `/account-data-notice`. They are not a compliance certification or a guarantee against claims. Have a lawyer review the final operator identity, business terms, liability clause, and actual deployment arrangements before offering the service commercially.

## Current law and sources

- India: read the [DPDP Act 2023](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf), [final Rules, G.S.R. 846(E)](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf), [commencement notification, G.S.R. 843(E)](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/c56ceae6c383460ca69577428d36828b.pdf), and [December corrigenda, G.S.R. 892(E)](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/12/3c7ebbae0e5456f493f486e6845df86b.pdf). The core notice, consent, fiduciary duties, and individual-rights provisions have an 18-month commencement period; consent-manager provisions have a one-year phase, while specified institutional provisions started on publication. The Gazette masthead says 13 November 2025, with a 14 November electronic publication code. **Planning inference:** prepare for the earlier dates, 13 November 2026 and 13 May 2027, and confirm the legal deadline with counsel. The corrigenda change wording and references; do not use the January 2025 draft as the final rules. Do not assume section 43A/SPDI duties have vanished before the relevant repeal commences.
- Current Indian duties also need review: the [2011 SPDI Rules (WIPO legal text)](https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/legislation/details/15063) include passwords and grievance handling; [CERT-In’s directions](https://www.cert-in.org.in/PDF/CERT-In_Directions_70B_28.04.2022.pdf) require covered entities to report specified incidents within six hours and keep specified ICT logs for 180 days in India. The security-log duty is separate from retaining all enquiries.
- EU/EEA: [GDPR text](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng) and [EDPB territorial-scope guidance](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/sites/default/files/files/file1/edpb_guidelines_3_2018_territorial_scope_after_public_consultation_en_1.pdf). Establishment, targeting services, and monitoring determine scope; a visitor's citizenship or a globally accessible page alone does not settle it. Review representative requirements and restricted transfers before onboarding affected customers.
- UK: [ICO privacy-notice guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/individual-rights/the-right-to-be-informed/what-privacy-information-should-we-provide/) and [processor-contract guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/accountability-and-governance/contracts-and-liabilities-between-controllers-and-processors-multi/what-needs-to-be-included-in-the-contract/). The latter is flagged for review following the Data (Use and Access) Act; check the current UK position before entering a UK contract. The DPA includes instructions, confidentiality, security, further-processor controls, rights assistance, incidents, return/deletion, and audit rights. It does not substitute for a completed international-transfer instrument.
- California: [regulator FAQs](https://cppa.ca.gov/faq.html), [current law/regulations](https://cppa.ca.gov/regulations/), and [updated revenue threshold](https://www.cppa.ca.gov/regulations/cpi_adjustment.html). Determine statutory coverage first, including the revenue, volume, and sale/sharing tests and relevant affiliated-business rules. The policy supplies conditional rights language rather than claiming that the CCPA automatically applies to this small service. Check other targeted markets before launch.

## What is implemented

- Separate, initially unchecked signup choices for adult/authorized terms acceptance and specified account-data consent. The server requires literal `true` for each and the current notice version; absent, false, string, or stale choices fail.
- New accounts store `termsAcceptedAt`, `termsVersion`, `accountConsentAt`, and `accountNoticeVersion`. No additional raw IP or device evidence is collected for this. Terms acceptance includes the published DPA, while account consent is not customer-form or marketing consent.
- The additive migration leaves old accounts' evidence `NULL`. **Existing users have not accepted these new terms.** Obtain a separately evidenced agreement/notice and any required consent before relying on the new contract for them; do not silently backfill acceptance.
- Public/footer, authentication, Settings, sitemap, and agent-resource links, plus a privacy step in the existing customer form-launch checklist.
- Public operator/provider/location disclosures are read from environment configuration at request time. The production signup screen and registration API reject new accounts until all required details are valid. A configured contact does not itself certify that contractual/operational duties are satisfied.
- `vercel.json` disables Vercel Git deployments specifically for `codex/privacy-and-terms`. Other branches keep their normal behavior. No deployment or live database migration is part of this change. See [Vercel’s branch deployment setting](https://vercel.com/docs/project-configuration/git-configuration).

## Required before publication or commercial onboarding

1. Fill the six public `LEGAL_*` settings in `.env.example` with verified facts: legal operator name, postal address, monitored privacy/legal email, grievance contact, actual hosting/database/backup provider identities, and processing/support countries. Do not invent a company, DPO, residency promise, or mailbox. Test the email and assign an accountable person. Preserve the final published notices/version in Git; bump `lib/legal.ts` for material changes and retain previous revisions. A metadata “last updated” date is not proof of acceptance.
2. Sign provider agreements and maintain a current further-processor/location register covering hosting, database, backups, mail, payment roles, and support access. Execute required EU/UK transfer instruments with completed annexes and assessments before transferring affected data; generic acceptance of this DPA is not sufficient. Implement the promised 30-day further-processor-change notice and objections process.
3. Establish a rights/grievance register. Verify identity proportionately, distinguish customer-controlled enquiries from operator-controlled accounts, log decisions, enforce applicable deadlines, and handle authorized agents, nomination, and US appeals where required. Current Indian SPDI grievances should be addressed within one month; future DPDP Rule 14 requires a published grievance period no longer than 90 days. EU/UK and US request deadlines are separate. Publish the applicable process before those duties apply. Arrange the Indian notice-language option and a consent-withdrawal route of comparable ease before the core DPDP provisions commence.
4. Implement and test a manual closure/erasure runbook now. Check enquiries, contact-email copies, notes, schema content, webhook jobs, failed/pending account mail, activity actor/subject IDs, saved search text, templates, invitations, tokens, and provider copies. Deleting a user cascades authored notes and can affect shared business records; first resolve ownership and preservation needs. Deleting one enquiry leaves detached activity, and archiving/cancellation/member removal does not erase a workspace. Redact metadata where required; do not treat IDs/hashes as anonymous. Account consent withdrawal must not depend on an owner's permission.
5. Set purpose-specific retention and backup expiry, with legal exceptions and deletion after restores. Current code does not automatically purge enquiries, activity, expired tokens/invitations, saved views, or rate-limit rows. Protect or clear delivery payloads and determine whether mandatory security-log rules require additional logs. Future DPDP Rule 6/8 retention provisions also require review when they commence. Do not advertise exact erasure deadlines, “all data encrypted,” or India-only storage until implemented and verified.
6. Maintain actual deployment safeguards: HTTPS, controlled staff access, protected secrets, suitable database/backup encryption, tested recovery, monitoring, confidentiality obligations, provider incident contacts, and a breach drill. Plan independent clocks: covered CERT-In incidents (six hours), applicable GDPR controller reporting (72 hours), processor notice without undue delay, and DPDP notifications/details when the relevant rules commence. Do not wait for every fact to be known before starting an incident response.
7. Tell customers to publish their own collection notice and keep proof of applicable consent. Generated HTML does not add their notice, age checks, marketing opt-in, or server-side consent history. A required checkbox can collect an answer but does not automatically establish adequate legal evidence. Do not process children's/sensitive data under the ordinary workflow. Paid onboarding needs checked checkout disclosures, renewal/cancellation/refund settings, tax handling, and consumer-law review.

## Verification and rollout

Run `pnpm test`, `pnpm lint`, `pnpm typecheck`, and `pnpm exec next build`. The normal `pnpm build` also applies database migrations; **do not use it for this no-deployment request**. Generate the client locally with `pnpm db:generate`. Before a later authorized release, apply the committed migration to the intended database and verify successful signup records both choices and versions. Existing records must remain intact with null consent fields.

`tests/ui/legal.spec.ts` can run against a local development server independently of the database-backed workspace UI suite. It checks legal pages at 320px and intercepts registration to verify required checkboxes and the submitted revision without creating accounts. The existing migration test additionally checks that no consent is invented for legacy users.

Validation on 8 October 2026: 44 unit checks and both focused browser checks passed; lint, standalone type checking, Prisma schema validation/client generation, and the production build passed. A local production-server check confirmed that absent operator disclosures return a closed signup screen and HTTP 503 from registration before database access. Mobile policy/signup previews were inspected. Database-backed registration and migration execution were not tested because the local Docker daemon was unavailable; no live database was accessed or migrated.

Do not merge/deploy this branch until the identity and operational items above have owners and the production disclosures are complete. Existing production accounts and form endpoints keep their prior behavior; this patch's readiness guard is for new registration, not a full compliance enforcement system.
