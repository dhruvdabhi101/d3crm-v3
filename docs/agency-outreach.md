# Agency outreach shortlist

Researched 3 October 2026. Product: [d3CRM](https://d3crm.vercel.app).

Start with agencies building enquiry-driven client websites. The proposed offer is a small website-form inbox with assignment, notes, follow-up dates, and email alerts, connected through a JSON submission endpoint. Agencies can evaluate it for their own enquiries or individual client workspaces. This is a fit hypothesis, not evidence that these agencies need a CRM or have missing leads. Their headcounts, budgets, and buying intent are unverified; the shortlist favors specialist agencies rather than claiming all are small.

## Before sending

- Replace `[Your name]` with your actual signature; the public URL is confirmed, but the signature name was not supplied.
- Verify registration, email verification, form submission, and notification delivery on the deployed site. A successful local build is not production verification.
- Reports, Activity, and attribution were not deployed when these drafts were written. The drafts deliberately focus on the core enquiry workflow; mention newer capabilities only after checking them on the deployed site.
- Do not claim native Webflow/Framer/Wix plugins, one-click integration, white-label d3CRM branding, file uploads, SMS, unlimited workspaces, compliance certification, or customer results. None was established here.
- Start with the first three agencies, one personal email each. Ask about one real project and the workflow they use today. Discuss price after fit is established; no invented price or free trial promise.
- Use one honest follow-up, then stop without a reply. Respect refusals; do not add recipients to automated campaigns.
- For Flowjoy, first discuss operational/data-processing requirements using dummy enquiries. d3CRM is not established as a replacement for its calls, chat, SMS, reporting, or contractual setup.

## What to learn from replies

Ask which sites generate enquiries, who responds, what happens after submission, which tools they use, and whether a separate inbox would create more work. For an interested agency, agree on one website, a clear integration scope, a dummy-data test, and actual pricing before a customer pilot. Validate the platform's custom-code capability rather than promising universal compatibility.
